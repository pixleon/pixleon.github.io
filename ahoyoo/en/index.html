How it works

Find treasure

Open chests by solving challenges

Swing the sword

Swing the sword at letters and numbers. You need to be quick with both your head and your hands

Free the key

The key is stuck. Slide the blocks away in the right order and get it out through the hatch

Load the cannon

Load the cannon and aim at letters to find the hidden word

Compete

Use what you know to beat the crabs

Upgrade your boat

Start with a worn-out sailing boat and build it into something much bigger

Watch out for the shark

The shark guards the water around the islands. Sail too far out and it comes after you

Build your island

Shape your island with sand, palm trees and bushes

Collect items

Collect gear that helps you on your adventure

Keep a logbook

Keep track of your progress

For parents

Nothing pushing purchases

Play and learn

Works without internet

No login

No account needed. Your child needs no email address, phone number or password.

Privacy and GDPR

  • The game saves on the device. Progress, islands and items stay on the device, not with us.
  • No personal data is collected in the game. We never ask for a name, email address, phone number or home address. Your child needs no account.
  • The newsletter is optional and for adults. If you sign up, your email address is stored with our provider Brevo on servers within the EU. We only use it to tell you when the game is released or has big news, and every email has a link to unsubscribe.
  • Anonymous statistics. The game only sends totals, such as how many islands have been cleared. They are added straight into everyone else’s and thrown away. No ID, no device and no profile is stored, so the numbers cannot be linked to a person, and there is nothing to hand over or delete.
  • No ad networks and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data with third parties. The site loads no outside scripts and sets no cookies.
  • Your rights. As we hold no data about your child, there is no account to request or delete. If you have signed up for the newsletter, you can have that data sent to you or deleted at any time — get in touch, or use the unsubscribe link.
